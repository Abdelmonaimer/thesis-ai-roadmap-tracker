Research question and article outline

Working question. For one supervised tomato plant inspection, how do a simple vision baseline and a VLA policy compare on task success, latency, memory and energy on an available Jetson?

Scope to confirm. One crop, one action, one camera setup and one device. Start with replay or simulation if hardware or robot demonstrations are unavailable. A policy that only labels images is perception; a VLA comparison needs an actual action, such as choosing an inspection viewpoint or moving a camera to it.

Article skeleton. Working title → problem and greenhouse scope → related work → gap to verify → research question → proposed methods → planned evaluation → limitations → references.

Evaluation. Use the same held-out trials for both methods. Log success criterion, hardware and power mode, warmup, median and p95 latency, peak memory, and joules per trial if measurable. Label which power rails you sampled. Separate real measurements from planned measurements.
